>>===== MODE =====>>
bibliography
<<===== MODE =====<<


>>===== DESCRIPTION =====>>
A date range rendered by date-parts that carry their own affixes instead of a
delimiter on cs:date (the pattern of the ABNT NBR 6023 styles: "maio/ago.
1989"). The suffix of the start's last differing part and the prefix of the
end's first differing part make way for the range delimiter; every other
affix stays. A range whose only difference is in a part the style does not
print is one date. The delimiter is the one of the largest differing part:
a range across months takes the month's "/" even when the days differ too.
<<===== DESCRIPTION =====<<


>>===== RESULT =====>>
<div class="csl-bib-body">
  <div class="csl-entry">May/August 1989</div>
  <div class="csl-entry">May 2000</div>
  <div class="csl-entry">3 May/5 August 2000</div>
  <div class="csl-entry">1973-1992</div>
</div>
